# Charm gate (v3, 18 Oct 2026) — what a @papercheques clip must look like

Vyx's taste, calibrated from the big TikTok accounts' Popular tabs and 16 Douyin accounts Vyx called "on charm" (full list: `library/accounts.csv`). Charm is necessary, not sufficient: Vyx's charming AE posts still stalled at ~6 likes/100 views.

## Checklist
1. **The air/light does the work:** wild weather (rain, snow, fog, storm, wind), blue hour (lights on, navy sky), or golden/pink glow.
2. **You're inside it:** handheld first-person, street level, by a window, walking. Not a far-off view, not drone.
3. **Movement is the point:** rain, snow, wind, waves, bubbles, light.
4. **One palette:** cool teal/grey/blue or warm amber/pink. Muted, film-like grade.
5. **Ordinary is fine:** cars, plates, pedestrians, plain streets.
6. **Shape:** vertical, real footage, payoff visible in frame 1. No sideways-filmed clips.

Core genre = Chinese "city mood": vertical POV of a city in rain/fog or at blue hour + sad-lyric caption + song. Each top account repeats ONE recipe.

## Vyx's verdicts
