# Character yield in the #jugg circle (local session, 15 Nov 2026; moved into the repo 16 Nov)
**Question (Vyx, 15 Nov):** which anime and characters yield the most in this niche?
**Method:** vidIQ `vidiq_tiktok_profile_videos` (5 credits per ~10 videos; captions carry anime/character tags). Yield = views ÷ that account's median. 103 posts across @smokinsasa7 (median 73.3K), @.sadaqatt (45.3K), @luhisaaaa (26.3K) and @stayvyxed (3.5K).

## Results
- **Romance/shoujo is steady:** Ao Haru Ride 3.8×; Your Lie in April 2.8× and above baseline on 3 of 3 accounts; Fruits Basket 1.9×. HxH 2.1× over 7 posts.
- **Shonen gives rare 1M hits but mostly underperforms:** JJK 0.66× over 10 posts, Demon Slayer 0.49× over 7.
- @stayvyxed's best: The Tunnel to Summer, 303K (87×).
- **Length:** median 17 s. It doesn't predict yield: posts at 2×+ and posts under 1× both have a 16 s median (n=103).
- **Audio:** PARTYNEXTDOOR "Recognize" did 2.6× and 2.8× on 2 accounts. Songs come from captions (the lyric, the artist tag, or "ac/ib @editor"). Most are underground or layered, and web search can't find them. Using another editor's audio with an "ac @sasa" credit is normal in this circle.
- **vidIQ limits:** outlier search goes by concept and can't filter by hashtag (60 results, only 1 tagged #jugg). No aspect ratio.
