**Question (Vyx, 17 Nov):** the Firefly Wedding edit flopped (490 views, 2.5 s average watch). Frame 1 isn't it (both circle blows open on black) and collabs are off. What else?
**Data:** public pulls 17 Nov (`research/data/`): @chequethetime 17 posts, @stayvyxed 104, @smokinsasa7 136, @.sadaqatt 209. "×" = a post's views ÷ the median of that account's previous 10 posts (posts under 2 days old left out).
**Scripts:** `research/scripts/anime_features.py`, `research/scripts/caption_types.py`.

## 1. Mechanics don't track views (438 posts, 3 accounts)
Spearman ρ between each feature and × inside each account:

| Feature | @stayvyxed | @smokinsasa7 | @.sadaqatt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loudness (TikTok field) | +0.10 | −0.08 | −0.01 |
| TikTok quality score | +0.18 | −0.04 | −0.17 |
| Length | +0.13 | +0.11 | +0.03 |
| Posting hour | −0.16 | +0.05 | −0.02 |

| Posts in the previous 24 h | +0.03 | −0.14 | −0.07 |
| Hashtag count | −0.01 | +0.08 | −0.04 |

Every |ρ| is under 0.2 and the signs disagree between accounts: noise. So export quality, metadata tricks, loudness, length, timing and cadence aren't where the views are. Our four November posts were served as the original file (12.6–19.8 Mbps, no lower tiers), but the one served at 215 kbps (Ao Haru Ride) flopped the same, so file weight isn't it either.

## 2. Caption voice is the biggest split in Vyx's own data
Claude labelled each @stayvyxed caption (labels in `caption_types.py`):
- **F, fan voice:** an opinion, question or reaction about a show, character or episode ("Is jujutsu kaisen anime of the year?", "Maki was moving different in the new jujutsu kaisen episode").
- **L, lyric:** a song line ("got me stuck in a dream", "5 in the am").
- **T, title:** "Show x Artist", a song title or an edit title.
- **O, other:** labs, collabs, thanks, meta.

| Caption | Posts | Median × | ≥1× | ≥3× | ≥10× |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fan voice | 18 | 2.11 | 13 | 8 (44%) | 4 |
| Title | 38 | 1.02 | 22 | 10 (26%) | 2 |
| Lyric | 29 | 0.42 | 9 | 1 (3%) | 0 |
| Other | 13 | 0.49 | 4 | 0 | 0 |

- **Without January 2026** (the JJK S3 cluster): fan voice median 1.36× (4/10 at ≥3×), lyric 0.39× (1/28).
- **@chequethetime agrees:** Feb fan-voice posts median 4.9K; since September 1 of 10 posts reached 1K (all lyric, meta or no caption).
- **Viewers react the same** (median per view on @stayvyxed): likes 14.5% F vs 13.7% L, shares 1.45% vs 1.41%, comments 0.23% vs 0.56%. So the gap is how far a post travels, not how much watchers like it. Studio search share: fan-voice posts 10–25% (JJK, Choso, AOT, Black Clover), lyric posts 0.4% and 6.3% (Ao Haru Ride, Firefly) [V Studio, n=6]. A likely route: names and opinions match what people search and tell TikTok what the post is about [est].
- **Caveats:** Claude's labels; lyric captions also go with romance/jugg edits, fan voice with shonen hype, so caption and content are tangled. Only a test that changes the caption alone can separate them.
- **The circle uses lyric captions and still blows,** but with 15–53K followers in the niche. A 90-follower account may need the caption to do the work their audience does [est].

## 6. Remakes beat originals in the niche (@.sadaqatt)
- Posts credited "rm"/"remake" (a new edit of someone else's edit): median 1.75× vs 0.92× for the rest (n=21 vs 182). A remake beats a non-remake 67% of the time (Mann-Whitney, p≈0.01).
- **Holds inside popular-shonen tags** (JJK, Naruto, One Piece, Bleach, Haikyuu…): 2.08× vs 0.99× (p≈0.02), so it isn't just the characters.
- **The big ones are remakes:** 1.8M Luffy (70.6×), 749K Sasuke (21.5×), 264K Rukia, 229K Gojo, 220K Choso (rm @sasaa).
- **@smokinsasa7** has 1 remake, so no test there.
- **Labs/collabs/parts lose:** @.sadaqatt 0.88× vs 1.07× (14% vs 34% at ≥2×); since Oct, all three circle labs/collab parts got 0.14–0.24×. That matches Vyx's call to drop collabs.
- **Ours:** one remake, "baby say its trueee // ac/rm @smokinsasa7" (Ao Haru Ride, 15 Nov): 416 views. Unknown which sasa edit or how close.
- **Since 1 Oct the circle's only ≥2× posts were both Ao Haru Ride:** sasa "and when i hold you in my arms" 313K (4.6×) and .sadaqatt "my only loooveeee" 95.6K (2.9×).
