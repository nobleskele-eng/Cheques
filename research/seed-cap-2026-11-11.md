The 4 generic #fyp #viral #views captions got 704–971. **Weak lead, n=2.** #fyp, #viral and #views carry no topic, so they can't help TikTok pick who to test on.
- **Served quality:** the 9 Nov post has 540p and 720p versions only (best 0.79 Mbps). The 10 and 12 Nov posts list only the original 4K file (43–46 Mbps) and no playback versions, so what viewers actually got can't be read from outside. Still 0 posts with a 1080p version.

## 2. Reading
- Across 51 posts we've changed sound (official, other accounts', proven), length (6–11 s), openings (tilt-up reveals p76/p77/p81), timing, edits and now captions. **None moved the ~700 band.** [sourced: self report 7 Nov + this pull]
- **Every post is another creator's Douyin footage.** TikTok's For You standards exclude reposted footage that adds nothing new, and 4 of ours were flagged unoriginal (p53, p58, p59, p62). A quieter penalty would fit our data, **but our own numbers argue against it:** p81 went up 0.6 days after its Douyin upload (creator had 420 likes, so it almost certainly wasn't on TikTok yet) and still got 811. Across the 7 mapped posts, Douyin age at posting (0.6–16 days) shows no link to views (fresher half median 720, older half 702). [sourced: log.csv + this pull] Own footage would be the only clean test, and it's ruled out (account keeps its "mysterious aura", Vyx 19 Oct). **Treat as untestable within the rules, not as the lead.**
- **A full account-level block is unlikely:** TikTok says it can make a whole account temporarily ineligible after repeated ineligible posts, and it notifies the creator. That would drop For You views to near zero, not a steady 700. [est from mechanism] Vyx can rule it out in a minute (app: Settings and privacy, Account, Account status).
- **Biggest data gap:** no post has Studio watch-time numbers (% For You, average watch time, % watched to the end). They are the only way to tell "the test audience swipes away" (fix the first second / quality) from "they watch but don't react" (fix the content or who sees it).

   - Same day, the local session checks which quality versions TikTok built (in-page item_list from our profile; no video pages). That tells us if the app path gets a 1080p version, which none of our 51 posts has had and 81–99% of network posts have.
   - **Pass mark [est]:** any one clears ~1,500 views (about 2× our 90th percentile, 977).
   - **If Vyx already used the app for the 10–12 Nov posts:** that test is done (704–971, no change). Then the next lever comes from the Studio watch-time numbers.
3. Keep 40+ min gaps so each post is a clean test.
