## Picks
1. **Studio screenshots (no install).** Overview + Viewers tab per post at ~24 h; Claude reads the images. This fills the biggest gap: watch time, % For You and country. We have Studio data for 5 of 58 posts and watch time for only 2 (p98, p99) [seed-cap §5, §8, §9].
2. **AniList GraphQL API (anime accounts).** Free, no key, `https://graphql.anilist.co`. It has an airing schedule (`timeUntilAiring`, episode) and a trending sort. Limit 90 requests/min, temporarily 30/min per its docs. Use: a weekly "what's airing/trending" list so edits land on interest spikes. p27's 4,688 views came from an Edgerunners 2 spike, 67% from Search [MASTER §1].

## Algorithm facts found
- **US TikTok is a separate system now.** The USDS Joint Venture closed on 22 Jan 2026. The algorithm is licensed to it and is being retrained on US data in Oracle's cloud [news]. So @sylvanism_ (US) and @papercheques (Canada) are probably judged by different versions [est].
- **For You eligibility.** TikTok's FYF standards page reads "Effective September 24, 2026" [tiktok.com, fetched 16 Nov]. The unoriginal-content clause lives in the Community Guidelines sections, not on that page. Third-party pages say reused content without creative edits has been ineligible since 13 Sep 2025 [web]. Fan edits add creative work, so the anime accounts are probably less exposed than straight reposts [est].
