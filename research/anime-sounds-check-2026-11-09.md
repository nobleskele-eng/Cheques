# Anime-release sound tracking: checked, not worth building (2026-11-09, cloud)

Question (Vyx): track anime releases (e.g. One Piece season, Fortnite collab) to get ahead on sounds, since the #1 tier A sound on `reports/sounds.md` is "original sound by Monkey.D.Luffy".

## Findings (TikTok metadata in `research/data/*_sounds.csv`, 7 network accounts, 2,914 sound uses)
1. **The Luffy sound isn't One Piece content.** "Monkey.D.Luffy" is the uploader's display name. TikTok matches the audio to a released commercial track (Spotify link in `new_sounds_2026-11-09.csv`). Sound created 2025-05-15; elsewhere557 first used it 2026-10-27, 530 days later, so no tie to a 2026 release.
2. **Anime-named sounds are rare:** 3 of 2,914 uses (0.1%), 2 of 984 unique sounds (Luffy ×2, "see you later eren" ×1 on kadzoo55871, 25.6K views). English keyword search on title + author, so non-Latin names would be missed.
3. **The network uses old sounds:** median sound age at use is 95 days. 6% are used within 7 days of creation, 18% at 7–30 days, 35% at 30–180 days, 41% past 180 days. Release timing doesn't drive what this niche picks up.
4. **The Monday sounds routine already caught it:** the Luffy sound was tier A on the 2 Nov list, the first run after its first network use (27 Oct).
5. Our one anime-named sound ("Thủ lĩnh anime mimi", 20 Oct 03:46 UTC): 164 views vs our ~726 median. n=1, not evidence either way.

## Decision
Proposed: no anime/release tracker for @papercheques. Keep the release-agnostic Monday sounds routine. Try the Luffy sound on a lite-PASS post (2 uses, both beat median, 31× median; one account only). Awaiting Vyx.
